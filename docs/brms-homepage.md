# BRMS home page

Published through the SharePoint page editor on 2026-10-16 at https://reedelsevier.sharepoint.com/sites/ELSBUProjects/BRMS.

The original page contained starter News and Microsoft tutorial links, a raw site activity feed, and the document library. The updated page replaces the starter News and activity web parts with a task-focused introduction. The document library and its existing folders are preserved.

## Layout and content

- Soft theme shading on the two-column introduction section.
- Heading: Keep resilience documents current.
- Purpose: find documents, confirm ownership and maintain review dates across BRMS.
- Two action buttons: Open governance register and Register a document. The latter opens the formatted existing NewForm page.
- Three-step review guidance: check the source, confirm ownership/frequency, then update Last Reviewed Date only after completion.
- Explicit readiness notice: production schedules remain off pending final acceptance.
- Lower section: approved 30/14/7/0-day cadence, weekly overdue follow-up and manager escalation from 14 days overdue.
- Support and handover: the two approved business contacts, all three flow links, notification history, run history and configuration.
- Source flexibility: documents can remain at their approved source locations; the register links to the current source. Source edits do not complete governance reviews.

The page distinguishes approved policy from current operating state. Both fallback contacts are approved, but multiple-recipient fallback deployment and validation remain pending. Existing content was edited through normal page versioning; no document library contents were deleted.
